I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at Kansas State University, advised by Prof. Doina Caragea, and a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant. My research focuses on semi-supervised learning, crisis informatics, social media data analytics, multimodal learning, and learning with noisy labels. My dissertation explores LLM-informed approaches for real-time emergency events using social media data.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a Software Developer at Trimble, Inc. in Chennai, India (2021–2023), and earned my B.Tech. in Information Technology from Kongu Engineering College. I have taught CIS 560 (Databases), CIS 300 (Data & Program Structures), CIS 116 (Intro to Python), and CC 590 (Intro to AI) at K-State.
