I am a Ph.D. student in the [Department of Computer Science](https://www.cs.ksu.edu/) at Kansas State University, advised by [Prof. Doina Caragea](https://dcaragea.github.io/), and a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant. My research focuses on semi-supervised learning, crisis informatics, social media data analytics, multimodal learning, and learning with noisy labels. My dissertation explores LLM-informed approaches for real-time emergency events using social media data.

Before my Ph.D., I worked as a Software Developer at Trimble, Inc. in Chennai, India (2021–2023), and earned my B.Tech. in [Information Technology](https://kongu.ac.in/kongu.ac.in/departments/it.php) from [Kongu Engineering College](https://www.kongu.ac.in/), Erode, India, in 2021. I also interned at Trimble and, virtually, as a computer vision intern at Bennett University. I have taught CIS 560 (Databases), CIS 300 (Data & Program Structures), CIS 116 (Intro to Python), and CC 590 (Intro to AI) at K-State.
